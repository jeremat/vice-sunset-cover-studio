Vice Sunset Cover Studio is an offline web app that turns your photos and a caption into multi-panel, GTA-style cover art — a Vice City sunset poster with a painted sky, retro sun, skyline and palm silhouettes, big gradient lettering, film grain and a vignette — exported as a PNG to share. Pick a layout (Solo, Duo, Trio, Grid or Strip), load up to four photos, frame each one by dragging it on the cover or with the zoom and position sliders, choose one of five grades (Vice Sunset, Neon Night, Golden Hour, Noir Neon, Palm Teal), then export in post, portrait, story or wide size. Everything runs in the browser with no network request, and your photos never leave your machine. All art is original — no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or fonts.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Add a photo to a panel, write your title, pick a layout and grade, and press Export PNG. No build step, no account, no upload.
